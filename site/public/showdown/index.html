HOW TO PLAY

CALLING PLAYS

Before every snap, pick 1 of 4 plays. On defense you're guessing their play. Guess right and your defense blows it up. The CPU can read your call too.

OFFENSE

Runs: you take the handoff, steer with the stick.
Passes: B cycles your target (yellow marker), A throws. Cross the line and you're a runner.
Getting tackled? Mash A to break free.

DEFENSE

You control the player with the yellow arrow. A dives (a sure tackle if you connect). B switches to the defender nearest the ball. Mash A while blocked to shed the blocker.

THE GAME

Four 2-minute quarters. 4th down brings up PUNT and FIELD GOAL. Ties go to college overtime from the 25.

KEYBOARD

Arrows / WASD to move · Space = A · Shift = B · Esc = pause
